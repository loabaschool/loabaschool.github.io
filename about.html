Our Journey & History

Loaba Nursery and Primary School stands as a polestar in Thoothukudi District, guiding every student passing by. The school proudly celebrates 30 years of educational service since its founding in 1996.

Our New Building & Campus Infrastructure

Loaba Nursery & Primary School was started in the year 1996, at the outskirts of Thoothukudi - Mudivaithanendal Village, with just 3 teachers & 27 students. Today, the school educates more than 400 students till grade 5, with 25 motivated teachers & boasts facilities like play equipment for children, a well-equipped computer lab & Smart board Classrooms.

The primary aim of this school is to encourage students to be responsible, self-reliant, give them a worldview to meet daily challenges, shape character & personality. The School plays a small part in removing illiteracy and provides our students an opportunity to break out from the cycle of poverty.

Our school believes that it is the responsibility of our entire community to come forward & educate the uneducated, specially the under privileged. No initiative will last long or achieve objectives without participation from all sections of our society.

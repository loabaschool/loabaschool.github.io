Designed for Young Learners

Loaba Nursery and Primary School is always adapting to the needs of our children and we strive to give them the best that we can. Each classroom is fully equipped with the necessities for learning, a computer, a class library, and chairs and tables tailored to fit the need and age of the children in that room.

Our school hall is multi-functional and is used for indoor P.E., featuring new climbing bars, ropes, and foldable tables for lunchtimes.

Our Library is in the process of regeneration at present but remains fully accessible to the children whilst the revamp takes place.

We have also recently installed a new kitchen and music room for the children to explore, cook, and get creative!

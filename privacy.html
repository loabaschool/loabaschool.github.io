Privacy Policy

How Loaba Nursery and Primary School collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Loaba Nursery and Primary School ("we," "our," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry through it. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form on this website, including:

  • Your name and your child's name (Admission Inquiry form)
  • Phone number
  • Grade or class of interest
  • Any message or notes you choose to include

We do not collect this information automatically — it is only submitted when you fill in and send a form.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our Admission Inquiry and Contact forms is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Process admission applications
  • Contact you regarding your child's enrollment

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services to operate certain features:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers form submissions (Admission Inquiry, Contact) to our email. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they handle this data in transit.
  • Facebook — our Reviews & Community section links to and may embed content from our official Facebook Page.
  • Blogger (Google) — our Latest News & Blogs section pulls publicly available post data from our official Blogger feed.

These third-party platforms may collect data according to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review separately.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Admission Inquiry form may include a child's name and grade for enrollment purposes only. We do not knowingly collect any other personal data about children through this website, and this information is used exclusively for admission processing by authorized school staff.

5. Data Storage & Security

Form submissions are delivered directly to our official school email via Formspree and are not stored in a public database on this website. We take reasonable precautions to protect the information shared with us, though no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies of its own. Embedded third-party content (such as Facebook widgets) may set cookies in accordance with their own policies when loaded in your browser.

7. Your Rights

You may request that we delete any information you've submitted to us by contacting us directly using the details below. We will act on such requests promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at loabaschoolmudivai@gmail.com or call our office at 9442522311.

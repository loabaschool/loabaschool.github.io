Why We Are

Loaba Nursery and Primary School is a pioneer English Medium School in Mudivaithanendal. The Trust is comprised of legends from different walks of life. The School is unique in its own way, instilling spirituality in young minds. We have a team of dedicated teachers, supportive parents, disciplined students, and well-wishers.

School Motto

"Labor Omnia Vincit" — Latin for "Labour conquers all things." Hard work embodied in diligence and perseverance leads one to achieve greater things in life. In whatever you do, do your best.

It is our hope that each student will approach learning with passion and perseverance; that they will form lasting friendships and understand the meaning of loyalty, sincerity and fellowship; that they will become socially responsible citizens who give of themselves for the greater good — and always be mindful of God's grace and presence.

Vision & Mission

🎯 Our Vision

To adopt a Student-Centric, Values-Driven approach in managing school discipline, nurturing socially confident and responsible students through Character Development — emphasizing the 5A's: Attitude, Aspiration, Agility, Adherence and Aesthetics.

🌱 Our Mission

To bring about the all-round development of students by blending our rich heritage with progressive methods of education, inspiring creativity to help students discover their innate talents and aptitudes.

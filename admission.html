Admission Process & Guidelines

Admissions are now open for the academic year 2026–2027 for Nursery up to Grade 5. We welcome parents seeking a student-centric, values-driven environment that blends academic rigor with character development.

📋 Enrollment Details

  • Classes Offered: Nursery, Primary (up to Grade 5).
  • Medium of Instruction: English Medium.
  • Required Documents: Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Copy, Passport-size photographs, and Transfer Certificate (if applicable).
  • Campus Visits: Parents are welcome to visit our campus in Mudivaithanendal between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM on working days.

For direct inquiries, call our main office at 9442522311.
